THE GAME IS
THE BEGINNING.

Kings Hockey Academy creates opportunities for young hockey players across Nova Scotia to learn, train, and grow. Through camps, skill sessions, and team programs, players can build their skills, hockey sense, and confidence in a positive, supportive environment. Our team programs include year-round training and serve players in Pictou County and the Greater Halifax area.

We believe development is about more than the next game. We encourage effort, teamwork, and enjoyment of hockey, helping players build their abilities and a lasting connection to the sport—on and off the ice.

Our goal is to help each player keep improving and reach their potential.

IMPORTANT INFORMATION

AN INDEPENDENT
DEVELOPMENT ACADEMY.

Kings Hockey Academy is an independent hockey development academy. It is not affiliated with or operated by a Minor Hockey Association, Hockey Nova Scotia, or Hockey Canada. Program participation is open regardless of association membership, subject to the requirements of each program.
