QUEBEC PREDATORS

TEAM
INFORMATION.

For players ready to challenge themselves, the Quebec Predators offer an elite spring hockey experience based in Quebec City. The program includes U9, U11, U13, and U15 age groups, with training throughout the year to support players’ continued development and prepare for spring competition.

Roster spots are available by invitation or through tryouts.
